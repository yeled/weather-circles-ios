New in 1.3

• The Weather Circle widget can now be pinned to any of your recent locations (or left following the app) — so four circles on the lock screen can be four different places.

• Forecast Circles, a second lock-screen widget: four mini station circles — cloud oktas, wind barb and weather — showing either the next eight hours in two-hour slots or the next four days, your choice when you add or edit the widget. It takes the rectangular slot (the widest the lock screen allows a single widget); add it twice, one of each, and the whole strip is circles.
